The problem

Skilled operators shouldn't be part‑time estimators.

In a busy season the office work loses to the shop floor, and money leaks out in predictable places.

Missed and late bids

Invites sit in the inbox, addenda get missed, and bids go out the night before, or not at all.

Slow takeoffs

SF/LF, slab counts, tile area and cabinet schedules get measured after hours. Bid volume is capped by your evenings.

Quotes that go cold

Quotes go out and nobody calls back. Winnable work turns into a lead graveyard.

Purchasing errors

Slab holds, sinks, tile lots and cabinet orders placed late or wrong mean remakes, rush freight and margin loss.

Drawings and submittals

Shop drawings compete with production for the same people, and late approvals push template and install dates.

Books behind

Bank recs months late, no job costing, AR not chased. You can't see which jobs make money.
